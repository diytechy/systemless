Scope and persistence

Movement only. No enemies or invented failure condition. Goal capture finishes the board. Bonus rings become ordinary dots when grabbed by the player; restart restores them. Original save files are not read. This pilot stores only hand color and completed-run count under systemless.clockwerx-html5.pilot.v1. Original scoring, music, campaign, original menus and save compatibility are omitted. Effects start off on every visit. Physical controller hardware remains unqualified.

Fidelity limits: rotation follows an approximate observed experimental 6 MHz reference, not independently qualified original timing. Dot collision uses a logical 48-pixel radius; artwork is shorter. Fonts, dot shadows and goal presentation are reconstructed. The hands use symmetric reconstructed curves and approximate gradients. The goal has the four triangular markers visible in the original reference; their six-state size/color shimmer follows the original sprite sequence at an estimated original-clock cadence. Hands rotate continuously around their original pivots; shading and tiny details differ from the bitmap frames. The collage background remains bitmap. Source-derived starburst flourishes circle the player and goal until the first successful player action, then fade; exact raster sparkle texture remains approximate.
