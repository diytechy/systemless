The pacing source explains why a limited diagnostic is reasonable: emulator.rs installs an instruction-per-tick budget from cpu_mhz/DEFAULT_VBL_HZ; run_frame bounds guest work by a wall-time allowance, catches up at most two ticks per paint and resets clock origin when backlog exceeds four. The observed backlog cap therefore does not mean all elapsed guest time was recovered. Worker_runtime.rs measures run_frame including guest execution/drawing/audio before snapshot/copy, so guestMs is not isolated instruction execution. The 13 ms rows match the healthy audio catch-up branch for 68K, but this trace lacks audio queue samples and cannot prove queue behavior. Worker requests coalesce while one is in flight and complete pending demand promptly; high browser callback count does not imply that guest time or unique game images kept pace. Repeated near-allowance work with small transport/copy cost supports testing whether instruction quota affects clock progress. It does not prove the cause or predict linear speedup.
