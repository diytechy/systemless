## BO record and chain contract

`BOStream2Spec`, CODE6:0934–09c0, reads grid, kind, then for version≥6 one flags byte and one discarded padding byte. The last serialized word is **not a difficulty mask**. Actual flags are two zero and six0x20; the actual starter has zero. `BOSpec2Info:0852` classifies kinds0–3 bomb,4–9 fuse,10–13 match from DATA A5−0x8e6. BO capacity is60. Match-bearing records create LI; LI capacity is5. Flags are tested as any nonzero byte for nonhuman ignition, not specifically bit0x20. The actual chain has one starter, six intermediate fuse records and one terminal bomb.

`BOActivateMatch:0b7c` requires armed=true. Humans may ignite any armed match; a nonhuman additionally needs nonzero flags. It synchronously calls SRActivate, hides all LI at that grid, requests sound11 and clears armed. There is no carried-match inventory. Even exhausted spark allocation consumes the match. Reset rearms classification0 records; LIReset restores hidden matches. The BO background is drawn from group1400 frame=kind at ROGetPixel(grid,+6,+6). LI uses group1600 at grid offset(+31,+31), three-frame loop beginning at3*matchSubtype plus9 when flags are nonzero. Match subtypes for kinds10..13 are0,1,2,2; entry leg tokens are4,8,1,2. LI clock is persistent unscaled100.

`SRFuse2Spark:92da` obtains the current BO grid/kind and the private per-kind path. Equality of supplied entry leg to the route's entry token starts at point0 with step+1; any other entry starts at last point with step−1. It is not a geometric nearest-neighbor search. On leaving a nonterminal path, `SRFrameProc:94a6` **increments the BO index** and passes the previous route's exit token, choosing offset+8 for positive direction or+12 for negative. The next record can reverse its own path. No bounds/continuity validation is performed in this path. Preserve serialized chain order and validate it before generation. The actual eight-record chain is structurally within these classes; its complete gameplay route is not qualified.

Original signed-byte path samples and transforms are private in fuse-paths.json:21 points for bomb kinds,48 for fuse kinds,25 for starter kinds. SRPosition:9216 applies vertical reflection48−v, horizontal reflection48−h, then optional swap, and adds ROGetPixel(grid,+6,+6). Do not replace the bends with uniform straight-line speed. This path is required input to any future adapter; no original path coordinates enter the helper or public report.

## Spark timing, collisions and capacity

SR has17 pooled sprites, group1500, logical layer3. Allocation selects the first invisible slot, initializes frame0/range0..3, snapshots the scaled MT client, and sets deadline=latched guest TickCount+MTAdjustPeriod(60). The scaled period is62: PAInit computes floor(47*4/3), then SRInit registers it as scaled. MTAdjustPeriod applies master-rate and difficulty percentages with integer division; do not replace this with a fixed one-second wall timer.

`SRFrameProc:940a` requires sprite visible and allocated. Each observed client-count change produces one callback, never catch-up. It first computes the next animation frame. In the waiting phase it compares a **fresh TickCount strictly greater than** the deadline; then sets range4..8 and frame4. Reaching frame8 on a subsequent callback sets range8..11 and travelling=true, but advances no path point in that callback. On every travelling callback, it erases the old6×6 background patch, advances one signed path index, crosses to the next BO record when needed, then positions the sprite. A terminal route invokes BOActivateBomb, hides the spark and clears its timer. SRReset hides allocated sparks and detaches their clocks.

Only travelling=true and visible SR sprites participate in SRCheckCollisions:964a; first match among17 slots wins. WACheckSparks requests human death reason15. Ordinary nonhuman WACollisions does not call this spark-death check. Source layer3 is before PU/DP layer11, explosion layer2, enemy layer1 and human layer0. Priority0 additions append; SU traversal caches the successor before callback. A newly appended sprite may be traversed later in the same sweep if an old successor exists, but its current-count snapshot suppresses a same-count callback. Preserve live traversal and synchronous allocation.

## Explosion callback and destruction order

`BOActivateBomb:0bfe` requests sound12 before finding a slot. It uses the first invisible of six pooled group1800 sprites. Exhaustion drops the explosion after the sound. It sets frame0, logical range0..10, step+1, unscaled150 clock/current-count snapshot, grid offset(+5,+5), and appends to logical layer2 only on first use. There is no radial BO-to-BO blast activation: chain propagation belongs to ordered SR traversal. Reset hides allocated explosions and detaches clocks; insertion flags persist until the next round start recreates used pool sprites.

`BOBombFrameProc:06c2` computes the next frame before effects. At callback frame3, stationary destruction order is **SP,DS,EL,GN,TR,WL,DO**. At every callback frame3..10 inclusive, moving destruction order is **DO,DP,WA**. At frame4, after those moving effects, it paints group1400 frame14 into the background and invalidates its rectangle, repeats stationary DO, then calls WLAnnihilateSprites. That last function at b536 is empty. At frame10 it performs moving effects before hiding and clearing the timer.

Group1800 has only10 descriptors, indices0..9. Frame10 is a callback sentinel. `SUProcessSprite`, CODE4:3ebe–3f38, invokes the callback before committing its descriptor pointer and clamps callback output to9 afterward. Therefore blast collision at callback frame3 uses **descriptor2**, and frame10 uses descriptor9. Do not derive collision from the just-computed visual frame. Keep original row outlines and RAW descriptor bounds; do not infer radius from smooth flames.

DOBombedMoving:49f6 scans dynamic dot records flagged+0x20 and uses sprite outlines. Stationary DO:4ac0 visits grid(r,c),(r+1,c),(r,c+1),(r+1,c+1), skipping dynamic records. `DOAnnihilate:4944` requires human-grabbable+0x11. For a bomb hit it adds runtime look0x40 only when neither look0x40 nor ring look0x200 is present, clears bonus look0x800/bonus byte if applicable, and updates sprite/background appearance. This **does not simply delete a pivot**. Burnt static appearance is group300 frames92+colorIndex, with separate group4100 secondary descriptors. Runtime look0x40 is unrelated to serialized raw0x40 protection. Collapsed human-unavailable rings are skipped; ordinary ring look prevents replacement with burnt look. DOReset restores the saved source specification; burnt state is transient.

DPBombed, CODE7:0c88, scans allocated droplets, hides collided sprites and clears clocks. WABombed, CODE6:d16e, scans WA slots in order, tests visibility and original outlines, hides each hit hand and sets its hidden-until timestamp to latchedTick+[300,600,900,1200][difficulty]. For slots0/1 it also invokes RODied with reason−1. Correction after the transfer audit: WAEnemyOneFrame:d98e–d9b4 tests saved WASpec type5 before hidden-until resurrection. Ordinary type1/2 enemies in Bombs do **not** automatically return on that timestamp; they remain hidden until the round reset. Assigning a deadline in WABombed alone does not imply resurrection. The normal round death barrier then resets transient BO/SR/LI/GO/DO/SP/WL state from source specs. Do not resume hazard simulation during the frozen effect/reset barrier.

## Walls, spikes, programs and goodies

`WLBombed:b91c` edits the existing wall topology: removes the cross at the bomb grid if present, then vertical tiny tiles at(r,c),(r+1,c), then horizontal tiny tiles at(r,c),(r,c+1). It paints removal frames4,2,3 respectively into the background, clears the active tile and corresponding WLInfo+4 availability, retaining topology definitions for reset. This is not source-outline collision against every wall. `WLShatter:b488` is a separate contact path: cross type2 computes segment collisions and destroys selected arms in order4,1,8,2; other types shatter whole. It is invoked from an Oil droplet path at8a34, not by the explosion callback. Child Bombs has no active PU, so the new required wall effect here is WLBombed. Retain the separate paths for higher difficulties.

SPBombed:9b3c scans spikes in record order and hides matching types using two per-type grid offsets, without a blast-outline test. For the actual kind2 spikes, offsets relative to blast are(0,0),(0,1). They restore on round reset. DS/EL/GN/TR callbacks still belong in the ordered contract, but those blocks are absent here and remain unsupported by this bounded helper.

Active programs require alphabet n/s/b/f. WAControlFromPC:caba consumes a one-based command and wraps even on failed action. Decisions occur at source cardinal frames when not sliding. n does nothing; s requests transfer, b requests bounce, f requests both. `WAAction:c710` reverses only on XOR(transfer,bounce); f therefore transfers without reversing. Permission/target lookup still precedes action. This is new beyond the earlier n/s/b-only case. Actual program strings remain private. Enemy class3 and f must be qualified before activation.

GO means a score/time **goodie**, not a match. `GOCreate:70ec` filters its32-bit mask and caps15. Source group1900 has17 frames, looping0..16 on unscaled200 at grid offset(+4,+4), logical layer15. Both serialized initial-frame requests are59, beyond the descriptor count: SUSetFrameIndex CODE4:3a98 ignores them, retaining the clone default frame. They must not become an invented sprite/type59.

`GOCheckCollisions:729e` returns the first visible colliding record. `GOActivate:71dc` ignores−1 or already-collected+8; requests sound22, starts a flourish with hide callback, starts a floating1000 score, latches collected, adds10 seconds, then SCAddBonus(actor,1000). It remains visible until the flourish callback, so the latch is essential. GOReset shows all and clears collected, without resetting the animation phase. WACollisions human order is spike,goodie,puddle,match,spark,starburst,then walls/doors. The function itself does not early-exit these side effects on a death latch. Nonhuman goodies/puddles are additionally mode-gated; nonhuman match checking has its actor gate plus BO flags. Do not award a carried match or turn the goodie into a required goal collectible.
